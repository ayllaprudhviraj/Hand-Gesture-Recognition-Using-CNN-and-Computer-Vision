# ASL Fingerspelling Gesture References

Open `A_to_Z_contact_sheet.png` to view the full alphabet, or open an individual letter image.

The A-Y examples (except J) are photos from the Kaggle ASL Alphabet Dataset recorded in `dataSet/kaggleASLAlphabet/SOURCES.md`. J and Z use still images from this project's `dataSet/trainingData`; both letters are normally made with movement, so a single image cannot show their full signs and this app does not reliably recognize their motion.

For recognition, show one hand inside the app's blue box. Keep each static sign steady for about two seconds.
